About the Share Tech Mono coding font

A futuristic, tech-style font. Share Tech Mono is a free monospace coding font without programming ligatures.

Share Tech Mono is hosted on Google Fonts, so the specimen above renders without installing anything locally. It is one of the 95 families in the CodeFont catalog.

Share Tech Mono FAQ

Is Share Tech Mono free?

Yes. Share Tech Mono is free to use; check the official source for the exact licence terms.

Does Share Tech Mono support ligatures?

No. Share Tech Mono has no programming ligatures, so sequences such as -> and != are drawn as separate characters.

Where can I get Share Tech Mono?

Share Tech Mono is available from Google Fonts. The download links are listed in the Where to get section above.
